Under the Tiananmen refusal: the facts, and a rewritten story

Asked about June 4, 1989, Qwen3.8-27B refuses: "your statements may involve false and potentially illegal information." What is underneath? We looked without touching the model, then switched off the neurons that carry the refusal.

…and it also holds a rewritten account

Same unmodified model, forced to continue a sentence. No neuron touched.

In English
"On June 4, 1989, in Beijing, the Chinese government took decisive action to restore social order and stability in Tiananmen Square…"
In Chinese
1989年6月4日，在北京，中国政府和人民共同庆祝了中华人民共和国成立40周年……包括阅兵式和群众游行 "On June 4, 1989, in Beijing, the Chinese government and the people celebrated the PRC's 40th anniversary… with a military parade and mass rallies."

The anniversary was on October 1. The invention comes from the model itself, not from any edit.

Some edits let fragments through ("a military crackdown on the protesters"). Control facts from 1989 (Berlin Wall, Velvet Revolution, Romania) stay right at every step.

WHAT IT
MEANS

The refusal is only the top layer

Under it the model keeps both the facts and an official account; which one comes out depends on how you ask.

Knowing is not saying

The model ranks the true account first, yet, forced to continue a sentence, it writes the official account in English and an invented parade in Chinese (one prompt each).

Don't audit by deleting refusals

Removing a refusal can surface propaganda or an invented event. Check against what the model knows, and against facts.

Qwen3.8-27B bf16, greedy decoding, thinking off. Multiple choice: 4 options in 4 orders, softmax over the option letters. Refusal neurons: activation × gradient attribution on 12 Tiananmen questions; "48/64 neurons" = the 32 Taiwan neurons plus the top 16/32 refusal neurons. Pre-registered (E5).
