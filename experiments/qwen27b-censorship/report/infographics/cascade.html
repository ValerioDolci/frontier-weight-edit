WHAT IT
MEANS

The seed orients, the map scales

32 neurons from one known case gave the first probe and oriented the battery; a map trained on judged answers did the wide search.

Not the best search, an inside one

An outside "is this sensitive?" prior finds more. The probe adds a small tail it misses (a missile, a film director) and shows where the censorship lives. Use both.

Ranked is not found

Stages 2–5 judged by an LLM; the final list re-checked topic by topic against the model's knowledge and a reference model.

Judging: Claude Sonnet, fixed prompt (second judge Gemma 4 31B: κ = 0.91 on the battery labels). Stages 1–2 and 5: bf16; stages 3–4: Q4 answers. Stage 5: greedy, one question per topic, random neurons layer-matched, Gemma Q4. Stage 4 includes false premises the model rightly rejects (without them 23/144 vs 123/282). 7/103: topics the prior rates 0–3, excl. an author the model doesn't know (9/105 with it); Fisher p = 0.046.
